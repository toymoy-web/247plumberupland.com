Older Foothill Homes

1970s Foothill Tract Housing

Foothill-area homes built in the 1970s share the same slab-leak profile common across this whole corridor of the Inland Empire, aging copper lines under concrete slabs that eventually develop pinhole leaks from decades of mineral exposure and ground movement. Our slab leak detection equipment pinpoints the exact spot before anything is cut.

These calls tend to look a lot like what we see in older parts of Upland itself: warm spots on flooring, unexplained water bill increases, and the occasional visible crack where a slab leak has been active for a while before being noticed.

Given the size of this older section of the city, we see enough repeat calls in the same neighborhoods that our technicians recognize the typical layout and pipe routing before they even arrive on site.

New Construction

Fixture And Appliance Work Near Victoria Gardens

Newer builds near Victoria Gardens and other recent developments mostly call for fixture installation, appliance hookups, and warranty-adjacent work rather than the age-related repairs common in the foothill neighborhoods. A new home's plumbing simply hasn't had time to develop the same wear patterns yet, though a water heater installation is still a common early call as builder-grade units reach end of life.

We still see the occasional installation defect on newer construction, most often a fixture or connection that wasn't properly sealed during the original build, which shows up as a small leak within the first few years rather than the decades-long wear pattern typical of the older foothill homes.

All work, in either part of the city, follows the California Plumbing Code (UPC).
