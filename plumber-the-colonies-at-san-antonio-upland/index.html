The Colonies at San Antonio is a planned community on Upland's north side, near the 210 Freeway. This page covers what is on record about it, what a plumber can and cannot assume, and how we handle common calls such as water heaters, softeners and disposals.

We are an Upland company with a line answered 24/7, so a call from the Colonies is a local call for us.

Verified Facts

What Planning Records Say About The Colonies

Planning Area

The Colonies at San Antonio Specific Plan covers about 440 acres. A 2001 environmental notice on file with the state places it between Campus Avenue and Cucamonga Creek, from 16th Street to 20th Street.

Scale

That notice described 1,150 residential units in the plan. A planned count is not the same as homes built, and we have not verified how many were completed.

Character

A local design firm describes the Colonies as a mixed-use planned community, so homes share the area with shopping and commercial uses.

Common Calls

Fixtures, Heaters And Water Quality

The plan dates to the early 2000s, so many homes here may be newer than those in Upland's historic districts. We do not know the age of any specific home, so we confirm it on site instead of assuming.

A tank water heater that is leaking or no longer heating is a common reason to call. We handle water heater replacement and, for owners who want one, tankless water heater service, including descaling.

Water quality is a separate question. The City of Upland publishes an annual water quality report with hardness readings, and we point homeowners to it instead of quoting a number from memory. If scale is building up on fixtures, a water softener or whole-home filtration system may help, and we explain the tradeoffs before you buy.

Kitchen calls often involve the garbage disposal. A jam is frequently about what went down the drain, and a unit that hums but will not spin can often be cleared without a replacement.

Before You Book

Association And Warranty Notes

Community Association Rules

If your home is part of an association, check its rules before changing exterior items such as a water heater location. We have not verified the rules that apply to any Colonies address.

Warranty Questions

If a home or appliance is still under a builder or manufacturer warranty, tell us before work starts. We document what we find, which helps with a claim.

FAQs

The Colonies at San Antonio Plumbing Questions

What area does The Colonies at San Antonio cover?

A 2001 environmental notice describes a specific plan area of about 440 acres between Campus Avenue and Cucamonga Creek, from 16th to 20th Street. Call if you are unsure whether your address is covered. We will help with the plumbing question regardless.

Do you install and service tankless water heaters?

Yes. We service tankless units, including descaling, and we help owners decide between tankless and a standard tank. The right choice depends on household size, gas supply and budget.

How hard is the water in my home?

The City of Upland publishes an annual water quality report with hardness readings, so check the latest one. We do not quote a hardness number for individual addresses.

My garbage disposal hums but does not spin. Is it dead?

Not necessarily. A jam is a common cause, and clearing it may restore the unit. If the motor is worn or the unit leaks at the seams, replacement makes more sense, and we tell you which applies.

Is emergency service available in the Colonies?

Yes. Our line is answered 24/7. For a burst pipe or active leak, close the main water valve first if it is safe to reach, then call.
