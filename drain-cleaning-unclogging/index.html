Most drain calls in Upland start small: a kitchen sink that drains slower each week, or a shower that backs up a little before finally clearing. By the time a drain stops completely, the blockage has usually been building for a while.

We clear the immediate clog first, then check whether the real cause is something that will keep coming back, tree roots, old pipe scale, or a collapsed section of line, so you are not calling us again in three months for the same drain.

The Cause, Explained

What's Actually Clogging Upland Drains

Grease, soap scum, and hair account for most kitchen and bathroom clogs, and a standard cable or snake clears these in one visit. That is the majority of our calls and usually the quickest to resolve.

Older sewer lines, particularly near downtown Upland and in tree-lined pockets close to Euclid Avenue, are more prone to root intrusion. Roots find their way into small cracks or joints in clay or older cast iron pipe, and a plain snake only punches a temporary hole through the growth rather than clearing it.

For a recurring clog in the same spot, hydro jetting cuts through both the blockage and the root mass along the pipe wall, which is why it holds longer than repeated snaking on the same line.

Signs To Watch For

Signs You Need Professional Drain Cleaning

Slow Draining Sinks

One slow drain is often local. Multiple slow drains at once usually points to a main line issue further down.

Gurgling Sounds

Air trapped behind a partial blockage. Worth addressing before it becomes a full stoppage.

Repeated Clogs In One Spot

A drain that clogs again within weeks of snaking usually has root intrusion or a structural issue, not just debris.

Sewage Odor

Can mean a blocked vent or a break in the line. Not something to wait on.

Method Comparison

Snaking Vs Hydro Jetting

Cable / Snake

  • Good for a single, isolated clog
  • Faster for simple debris blockages
  • Does not clear root mass fully
  • Lower cost for a one-time clog

Hydro Jetting

  • Clears root intrusion and pipe-wall buildup
  • Better for recurring clogs in the same spot
  • Restores fuller pipe diameter, not just a channel through it
  • Recommended before a camera inspection on older lines

FAQs

Common Questions

Can a clogged drain be an emergency?

Yes, especially a backed-up toilet or a main line stoppage affecting multiple fixtures. Our emergency line runs 24/7 for exactly this.

Do you use cameras to find the clog?

For recurring clogs or main sewer line issues, yes. For a simple single-fixture clog, a snake is usually enough.

How often should hydro jetting be done on an older line?

For lines with a history of root intrusion, roughly every 12 to 18 months keeps buildup from returning to a problem level.

Will snaking damage my pipes?

A properly sized cable used correctly should not damage pipe. We match the equipment to the pipe material and diameter.
