A running toilet is usually a fill valve or flapper issue, quick and inexpensive to fix. A toilet rocking slightly or leaking at the base is a different problem, often a failed wax ring, and left alone it can lead to water damage on the subfloor below.

We handle the full range on this call: running toilets, clogs a plunger can't clear, wax ring replacement, and full toilet replacement when a unit is cracked or simply outdated.

The Cause, Explained

Common Toilet Problems We See In Upland

A running toilet almost always traces back to a worn flapper or a fill valve not shutting off completely, both inexpensive parts that are quick to replace. Left unaddressed, a running toilet can waste hundreds of gallons a month.

A toilet that rocks slightly or has water pooling at the base usually means the wax ring seal has failed. This is a common issue in older Upland homes where a toilet hasn't been reset in years, and it needs to be fixed before it causes subfloor damage.

Older toilets in homes built before the 1990s often use significantly more water per flush than current models, which is worth factoring in if a repair versus replacement decision is close.

A toilet that clogs frequently even with normal use sometimes points to a partial obstruction further down the line, or in some cases simply an older low-flow design from the mid-1990s that never flushed well to begin with.

Signs To Watch For

Signs You Need Repair Or Replacement

Running Or Refilling On Its Own

Usually a fill valve or flapper issue, a quick fix.

Rocking Or Water At The Base

Often a failed wax ring seal that needs to be replaced.

Frequent Clogging

Can mean a partial blockage further in the line, or simply an older, low-flow design that struggles.

Visible Cracks In The Bowl Or Tank

Not repairable. Full replacement is the only safe option.

Replacement

When A New Toilet Makes More Sense

A cracked bowl or tank can't be safely repaired. Beyond that, an aging toilet using significantly more water per flush than a current low-flow model is sometimes worth replacing on cost grounds alone, especially with a household that flushes frequently.

FAQs

Common Questions

Can you fix a running toilet the same day?

Yes, this is one of the fastest, most common repairs we do, usually completed within the same visit.

Is a wax ring leak serious?

It should be addressed promptly. Left long enough, it can damage the subfloor beneath the toilet, turning a simple fix into a bigger repair.

Should I repair or replace an older toilet?

If the bowl or tank is cracked, replacement is the only option. Otherwise it depends on the toilet's age, water usage, and how often it needs repairs.

Do you install comfort-height toilets?

Yes, we install standard and comfort-height models from all major brands.

How long does a toilet installation take?

Most installations, including removing the old unit, take one to two hours.
