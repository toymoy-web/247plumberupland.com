Newer Homes

Early-Life Problems, Warranty Questions And Attached Homes

In a recently built home, a plumbing problem in the first years is more often an installation issue or a defective part than ordinary wear. We find the cause, document what we find and tell you plainly whether it looks like a warranty matter. Whether a builder warranty applies is for you and the builder to decide.

Townhomes and other attached homes add a second concern, because a leak can reach a neighbor. Know where your main shutoff and any unit shutoff are before you need them. If water is already running into another unit, shut off the water first and then call.

Common calls here include water heater replacement when a unit fails or no longer suits the household, leak detection for unexplained moisture, toilet repair and installation and faucet and sink repair. In an emergency, our burst pipe repair crew responds around the clock.

Attached-Home Basics

Three Habits That Limit Damage

Locate Your Shutoffs

Find the main valve and each fixture shutoff while things are calm. It takes ten minutes and limits damage later.

Report Shared Leaks

If water crosses into a neighboring unit, tell the association or building manager right away.

Keep Records

Photos and dates help with a warranty or insurance claim. We note what we find on every visit.

FAQs

Sycamore Hills Plumbing Questions

Where is Sycamore Hills?

Local listings place it in north Upland, off the 210 Freeway along Baseline Road. If you are unsure whether your address is in the area, call and we will help with the plumbing question either way.

My home is new. Why am I having plumbing problems?

Early problems are more often a defective part or an installation issue than wear. We find the cause and document it. If a warranty may apply, contact your builder or manufacturer too.

What should I do if a leak reaches my neighbor?

Shut off the water to your unit, call us, and notify your neighbor and the association or building manager. We document where the leak started, which helps everyone sort out next steps.

Do you replace water heaters in townhomes?

Yes. Placement, venting and access vary by home, so we check those on site and explain the options before we start.

Is emergency service available in Sycamore Hills?

Yes. Our line is answered 24/7. Close the main water valve if you can reach it safely, then call.
