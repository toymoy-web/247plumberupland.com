Upland 24/7 Plumbing handles the full range of residential and commercial plumbing work across Upland and the surrounding area, from emergency repairs to planned installations.

Services below are grouped loosely by category: water heaters, drains and sewers, leak detection and repair, fixtures, water treatment, and commercial work. If you're not sure which one matches your issue, call and describe the symptom rather than guessing from a list.

Every service is quoted upfront after a real diagnosis, whether it's an emergency call or a planned installation. We don't quote sight-unseen, and we don't add charges beyond what was confirmed before work started.

A handful of these services, water damage restoration and mitigation specifically, use a separate dedicated line since they cover a different kind of work than standard plumbing repair. That number is listed on those two service pages directly.

For a planned installation, whether that's a new water heater, a bathroom fixture upgrade, or a whole-home repipe, we walk through options and tradeoffs before committing to a scope, rather than defaulting to whatever's fastest to install.

If you don't see your exact issue named below, call anyway and describe the symptom. Most plumbing problems fall under one of these services even when the symptom itself doesn't obviously match a service name.

What to check before you call

  • If it's an active leak or burst pipe, shut off the main water valve if you can safely reach it
  • If water is near outlets or a panel, turn off electricity to that area before doing anything else
  • For a suspected gas smell, leave the area and call your gas utility's emergency line first, then call us
  • Note roughly when the problem started and which fixture or area is affected, it speeds up diagnosis
