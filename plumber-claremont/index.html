The Core Problem

Root Intrusion Is Claremont's #1 Plumbing Issue

Tree roots find their way into small cracks or loose joints in older clay or cast iron sewer pipe, and once inside, they expand steadily over years. A standard cable clears the immediate blockage but leaves the root mass in place, which is why the same drain keeps clogging every few months, and why a one-off drain cleaning visit alone often doesn't hold.

Hydro jetting addresses this at the source rather than just punching a temporary channel through it, which is why we recommend it specifically for Claremont properties with a repeat-clog history.

A camera inspection before any jetting or repair work confirms whether we're dealing with root intrusion alone or whether the roots have already caused a crack or offset joint that needs a more involved sewer line repair.

Housing Stock

A Mix Of Historic And Mid-Century Homes

Claremont's housing ranges from early-1900s Craftsman homes near the Colleges to mid-century tract housing further south, which means pipe material and age vary more here than in a single-era neighborhood. Older Craftsman-era homes in particular are more likely to still have original clay sewer lines.

The mid-century tract sections further from the Colleges tend to see fewer root-intrusion calls simply because the trees along those streets are younger and less established, though the pattern still shows up as the neighborhood ages.
