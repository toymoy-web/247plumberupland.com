Historic Downtown Upland is the city's older commercial core, with shops, restaurants and offices, and a city-recognized Old Town District. This page is for business owners and property managers who need plumbing help, including after hours.

Our Upland shop and 24/7 line mean downtown is local to us. A blocked restaurant line at close or a burst supply in a vacant unit gets the same dispatch as any home emergency.

Verified Facts

What The City Records Say About The Old Town District

Old Town District

The City of Upland describes its Old Town District as commercial, with a period of significance of 1900 to 1930. The listed boundaries are A Street, First Avenue, C Street and Third Avenue.

Planning

The city lists a Historic Downtown Upland Specific Plan among its specific plans. We have not reviewed its provisions for plumbing work and do not cite them.

Mixed Use

Not every property near downtown is commercial. Some homes sit nearby, and our home service pages apply to them.

Commercial Needs

Grease, Shared Lines And Scheduled Work

Restaurants and cafes put grease, food solids and heavy daily use on their drain lines. A grease interceptor that is not serviced on a schedule can back up at the worst time. Our hydro jetting service clears the line itself, and we can set a service interval that matches your actual use.

Buildings with more than one tenant can share a drain or supply line. When a backup appears, finding which line it comes from decides who is responsible, so we run a camera and document the path before work. Our sewer line repair crews use that record.

Irrigation and some fixtures need backflow protection, and many water providers require periodic testing. Annual backflow testing for commercial properties can be scheduled so it does not interrupt business.

For ongoing needs, our commercial plumbing work includes planned maintenance, so a property manager is not calling only after something fails. Our drain cleaning service for restrooms and kitchens fits into the same plan.

Be Clear On The Limits

What We Have Not Verified

We have not verified the current tenant mix, building ages or plumbing layouts of downtown properties. Historic district status refers to a building's history, not to its plumbing, so we confirm conditions on site. Check with the City of Upland before work that affects a building's exterior.

FAQs

Historic Downtown Plumbing Questions

What is the Old Town District?

The City of Upland describes it as a commercial historic district with a period of significance of 1900 to 1930, bounded by A Street, First Avenue, C Street and Third Avenue.

Do you service restaurant grease lines downtown?

Yes. We clear grease-heavy drain lines with hydro jetting and can set a recurring interval based on how much your kitchen actually uses.

Who pays when a shared line backs up in a multi-tenant building?

That is between the owner and the tenants, and we do not decide it. We run a camera and document where the problem starts so the right party can act.

Can you schedule work outside business hours?

When practical, yes. Planned jetting, backflow tests and fixture work can be set for off-peak times. Call to talk through your hours.

Do you answer after-hours calls downtown?

Yes. Our line is answered 24/7. If water is flowing, close the nearest shutoff first if it is safe to reach, then call.
