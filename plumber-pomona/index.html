Pomona sits just west of Claremont and Montclair on the Los Angeles County side of the county line, and it has one of the largest concentrations of pre-war homes in the Inland Valley. Three of its five historic districts are residential, with more than 1,300 homes dating back to the 1800s listed among them, so plumbing from the 1890s through the 1940s is a normal part of a service call here.

What To Know Before You Call

Pre-War Homes, Original Pipes

Historic Districts

Lincoln Park, Wilton Heights, And Hacienda Park

The Lincoln Park Historic District, bounded roughly by Garey, Towne, and McKinley avenues, holds more than 800 structures, mostly single-family homes from the 1890s through the 1940s, with Victorian styles near Holt Avenue and Craftsman and revival styles to the north. Wilton Heights, west of Garey and north of Holt, spans the late 1800s to the 1950s, and Hacienda Park, designated in 2003, covers nearly a century of the city's building.

Homes this old were built before modern plumbing codes. We commonly find original galvanized supply lines narrowed by decades of scale and cast iron or clay drains that crack and take on roots. Because these homes are valued for their original character, we plan repairs to disturb as little finish work as possible and lay out the options, spot repair, rerouting, or a full repipe, before any wall is opened.

Beyond The Districts

Mid-Century Tracts, Rentals, And The Cal Poly Area

Outside the historic districts, Pomona's housing shifts to 1950s and later tract homes, apartments, and rentals, with a large student rental market on the east side near Cal Poly Pomona and the Fairplex fairgrounds close by.

Rentals and multi-unit buildings generate steady calls for drain cleaning, water heater replacement, and shared-line backups. Landlords and property managers can set up a standing arrangement so a recurring problem gets a recurring fix.
