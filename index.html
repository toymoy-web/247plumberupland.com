When To Call Right Away

What Counts As A Plumbing Emergency

If water is spreading, sewage is backing up or you smell gas, do not wait for business hours. Shut off the water at the main valve if you can reach it safely, then call our emergency line.

Sewage Or Drain Backups

Water coming up in a tub, shower or floor drain, or several drains backing up at once.

Active Leaks

Water in a ceiling, a wall or a garage, or a meter that spins with everything off.

Water Heater Failure

A tank leaking onto the floor, or no hot water at all.

Burst Or Cracked Pipes

Spraying water, a broken valve or a pipe failing inside a wall.

Gas Smell

Leave the building, call 911 and your gas utility, then call us for the repair.

Hot Floor Or Slab Leak

A warm spot on the floor or running water sounds with every fixture off.

Not sure if it qualifies? Call us and we will tell you honestly whether it can wait for a same-day visit or needs a plumber now.

Local Upland Plumbing

Built For Upland's Water And Housing

Water Hardness

Hard water can shorten water heater life and scale up tankless units. The City of Upland's 2025 water quality report lists average total hardness at 120 ppm, with readings from not detected up to 340 ppm. Our tankless service includes a real descale, not just a rinse.

Mixed Housing Ages

From downtown Upland's early-1900s Historic District to 1960s North Upland ranch homes to newer construction near Sycamore Hills, pipe material and failure points vary block to block.

Hillside & Pool Properties

San Antonio Heights and other hillside lots often carry long irrigation and pool-equipment runs that add leak points beyond the house itself.

Built To Code

All repairs and installations follow the California Plumbing Code, the state's adopted version of the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), not just manufacturer guidelines.

Upland sits at the base of the San Gabriel Mountains in San Bernardino County, and the city's plumbing calls reflect that geography as much as its age. Hillside lots in San Antonio Heights deal with pressure swings from long supply runs, while flatter North Upland streets are more about original 1960s pipe finally reaching the end of its service life. We size the fix to the actual property, not a generic checklist.

Because hardness minerals are a factor here, we treat tankless descaling and water softener conversations as a normal part of a water heater visit, not an upsell. A homeowner who mentions rising energy bills or a unit that seems to be working harder each year is usually describing scale buildup, and it's worth ruling that out before assuming a bigger repair is needed.

Upland Neighborhoods

Neighborhoods And Landmarks We Serve In Upland

Upland is a city of distinct pockets, each with its own housing age and its own plumbing pattern. Here is how we think about the parts of town we are called to most.

Historic Downtown And Euclid Avenue

Historic Downtown sits just off Euclid Avenue, the 200-foot-wide boulevard laid out by the Chaffey brothers and listed on the National Register in 2005. The Euclid Avenue Historic District holds homes built between 1895 and 1936, and Upland has nine designated historic districts in all, so original galvanized pipe and old drain lines are a regular find and sometimes a case for a repipe.

Foothill Boulevard And Historic Route 66

Foothill Boulevard is the old Route 66 through Upland, with the Madonna of the Trail statue at Euclid and the 1947 Grove Theater along the way. The shops, restaurants, and offices on this corridor are the kind of properties that need commercial plumbing help, from grease lines to annual backflow tests.

Cable Airport And The Mid-Century Neighborhoods

Cable Airport, a privately owned airfield about two miles northwest of downtown, anchors an area of established neighborhoods. Homes from the 1950s and 1960s here, many of them single-story ranches and split-levels, are now old enough that original supply lines and water heaters are common reasons for a call.

North Upland And Sycamore Hills

North of Foothill Boulevard toward the 210 Freeway, older ranch homes sit alongside newer building. Sycamore Hills, a master-planned community straddling Upland and Claremont at the 210 and Baseline Road, brings newer homes where calls are mostly installs and early-failure parts, including water heater replacements.

The Colonies At San Antonio

The Colonies at San Antonio, a master-planned community along Campus Avenue at the 210 Freeway, and the Colonies Crossroads shopping center beside it make up much of Upland's newer north side. Homes here are newer, so softener setups, fixture work, and replacement parts lead the calls.

The 1980s And 1990s Tracts

Two-story homes from the 1980s and 1990s fill many of the tracts between Foothill Boulevard and the 210. Tracts of that era often used polybutylene supply pipe, so a quick check is worthwhile if a home has never been repiped, and the water heater in these homes has often been replaced once already.

San Antonio Heights And Mt. Baldy

San Antonio Heights climbs toward the base of Mt. Baldy at the top of the Euclid Avenue median trail. Custom hillside homes on larger lots mean longer supply runs and more irrigation, and the community has its own page: San Antonio Heights plumbing. Calls from the mountain community of Mt. Baldy, further up the road, are handled case by case.

San Antonio Regional Hospital Area

San Antonio Regional Hospital is Upland's largest employer, and the medical offices, clinics, and restaurants around it and along Foothill Boulevard depend on reliable, code-compliant plumbing, backflow testing, and a fast response when something fails after hours.
