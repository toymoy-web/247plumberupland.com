About Upland 24/7 Plumbing

A licensed plumbing service provider based in Upland, CA, covering the surrounding western San Bernardino County area.

Who We Are

Upland 24/7 Plumbing is an independently owned and operated plumbing service provider based in Upland, California. We handle everything from emergency repairs to planned installations for homeowners and businesses across Upland and the surrounding cities.

Our technicians are licensed to work in California and background-checked before dispatch. Every job starts with a real diagnosis and an upfront price before any work begins, no exceptions.

What We Do

We cover the full range of residential and commercial plumbing work: water heaters, drain and sewer service, leak detection, fixture repair and installation, gas line work, and water treatment systems, among others. Our full list of services is available on our services page.

We also handle water damage restoration and mitigation as a separate, dedicated service, since that work requires different equipment and expertise from standard plumbing repair.

For commercial customers, we handle grease trap maintenance, backflow compliance testing, and scheduled maintenance plans built around business hours rather than a generic calendar.

Where We Work

Our home base is Upland, CA, and we regularly serve the surrounding cities and neighborhoods in western San Bernardino County, including San Antonio Heights, North Upland, Claremont, Montclair, and Rancho Cucamonga. See our service area page for the full coverage map.

Because we work across a range of housing ages and property types in this area, from downtown Upland's early-1900s homes to newer construction near Rancho Cucamonga's Victoria Gardens, our technicians are used to diagnosing a wide variety of plumbing systems rather than assuming a single generic layout.

Our Approach

We diagnose before we quote. That means a technician identifies the actual cause of a problem before giving you a price, rather than guessing or upselling based on the easiest assumption. Pricing is flat and confirmed before any work begins.

We also try to be honest about repair versus replacement. If a repair genuinely makes more sense than a full replacement, or vice versa, we say so, even when one option costs less than the other.

Licensing & Compliance

Upland 24/7 Plumbing is a California CSLB C-36 licensed plumbing contractor. All work follows the California Plumbing Code, the state's adopted version of the Uniform Plumbing Code, including permitting and inspection requirements where applicable.

Availability

Our emergency dispatch line is answered 24 hours a day, every day of the year, including holidays. For non-emergency scheduling, our office hours run 7am to 7pm daily.

Pricing Philosophy

We give a flat price after diagnosing the actual problem, not before. That means the price you're quoted on-site is the price you pay, with no add-on charges once work has started unless something genuinely different is found once we're inside the wall or under the slab, and even then we stop and confirm with you before continuing.

For non-emergency estimates, we don't charge just to come look and quote a job. Emergency dispatch carries its own upfront pricing structure, explained clearly on the call before a technician is sent, so there's no surprise between what you were told on the phone and what you're asked to pay.

This approach means occasionally quoting a job as more involved than a customer expected, but we'd rather have that conversation upfront than after work has already started.

Working With Property Managers And Businesses

Beyond individual homeowners, we regularly work with property managers, landlords, and commercial tenants across our service area. That includes providing the documentation many property managers need for maintenance records, insurance, or lease compliance, and scheduling non-emergency work around business hours rather than defaulting to whatever slot is next available.

For multi-unit properties specifically, we coordinate directly with the property manager rather than requiring every individual tenant to schedule separately, which keeps repairs moving faster when a shared system like a water heater affects more than one unit.

We also keep a record of past work at properties we service regularly, which speeds up diagnosis on repeat calls since we already know what's been repaired or replaced before.

A Note On Our Service Area

We built this business around Upland specifically, which means our technicians know the local housing stock, from downtown's early-1900s Historic District to the newer construction near Sycamore Hills, well enough to arrive with a reasonable idea of what they're likely dealing with. That local knowledge extends to the surrounding cities we regularly serve, each of which has its own distinct plumbing profile shaped by its housing age and geography.

If your address falls outside our usual coverage, call anyway. We'll tell you honestly whether we can get to you and how soon, rather than leaving you guessing.
